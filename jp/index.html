What could go wrong

Biotechnology investing has real risk. We explain risks in plain language.

  • Clinical delays can push timelines and lower short-term momentum.
  • Regulatory changes can delay approvals and shift expected launch timing.
  • Funding pressure can affect smaller teams during slow capital periods.
  • Market mood can move prices faster than business fundamentals.

How we reduce risk: broad opportunity spread and strict milestone checks.
